To my Luna, 💗

Happy first monthsary, love! 🥹💗

Can you believe it's already been one month with us? Maybe one month sounds short, but I'm really happy that I get to call you mine and that I get to share these little moments with you. Even if we're far from each other, you still make my days feel special.

I know I'm not perfect. I'm makulit, sometimes matigas ang ulo, and our body clocks are different because I usually sleep late. There may be times when our schedules don't match, when you want to rest and I'm still awake, or when we can't spend as much time together as we want. I'm sorry for the times that might make you feel like I'm not giving you enough time or attention.

But love, please know that I'm willing to learn and do better. I can't promise that I'll get everything right all the time, but I can promise that I'll keep trying, listen to you, understand you, and make an effort for us. I want us to talk things out instead of giving up, and I want you to feel loved even on ordinary days.

Thank you for being my Luna. Thank you for staying, for making me smile, and for being someone I look forward to talking to. You're special to me in ways that are hard to explain, but I hope I can show you through my actions—not just my words.

Even if I'm stubborn sometimes, please remember that my heart is serious about you. I want to be by your side through happy days, tiring days, misunderstandings, and all the little things in between. I'll keep choosing you, respecting you, and doing my best to grow with you.

Distance may keep us apart for now, and our sleeping schedules might not always cooperate 😭, but you still have a place in my day and in my heart. I hope this is only the first of many monthsaries we get to celebrate together.

I love you so much, love. More than this little website can say. Happy first monthsary to us. Here's to more calls, more laughs, more memories, and one day, more moments where I can hold you instead of just missing you through a screen. 🫶

Always your Dawn ☀️
